Meet your stylist

The studio feels like home, because it is one.

Tucked away in Manteca, the studio is all warm wood paneling, worn brick, and afternoon light — a far cry from a busy salon floor. When you sit in the chair, you're the only appointment that matters.

Marissa has spent over a decade behind the chair, with a soft spot for lived-in color, romantic waves, and haircuts that grow out as beautifully as they start. Every visit begins with a real conversation and ends with hair you can actually recreate at home.
